Who is Delta Sigma Pi?

Delta Sigma Pi prepares business students for successful professional careers, but also instills values of service and principled leadership that characterize Deltasigs in all phases of life. With more than 300,000 initiates, our members make a difference in their workplaces and communities throughout the United States and around the world.

The Alpha Kappa Chapter at UB

Bringing Delta Sigma Pi's mission to life at the University at Buffalo — season after season.

  1. 1907

    Delta Sigma Pi Founded

    The national fraternity is established at NYU's School of Commerce, Accounts and Finance — the start of the championship.

  2. 1925

    Alpha Kappa Chartered at UB

    Alpha Kappa Chapter is installed at the University at Buffalo on May 9, 1925.

  3. 2000

    Notable Milestone

    The City of Buffalo officially named November 7, 2000 "Alpha Kappa Chapter/Delta Sigma Pi Day," with the provision of Brother Ray Matthew, AK1394.

  4. Today

    Racing Forward

    Alpha Kappa continues to build the next generation of business leaders at UB — on and off the track.
