Know Before You Go

Technical Regulations

Here's exactly what each dress code means for Alpha Kappa events — so you always know what to wear.

Wear what you have and what you feel comfortable in. While some events may have recommended attire, we understand that not everyone has access to suits, ties, dress shoes, or other professional clothing. We would always rather have you show up in casual attire than not show up at all. Your interest in Delta Sigma Pi matters far more than what you're wearing.

Business Professional

Wear

  • Full suit (matching jacket & pants/skirt), or blazer with dress pants/skirt
  • Dress shirt or blouse with a tie
  • Closed-toe dress shoes
  • Minimal, professional accessories

Business Casual

Wear

  • Button-down shirt, blouse, or nice sweater
  • Slacks, chinos, or a knee-length skirt/dress
  • Loafers, flats, or clean casual dress shoes
  • A blazer is optional but always a safe upgrade

Casual

Wear

  • Jeans or joggers with a clean shirt
  • Sneakers or comfortable shoes
  • Whatever you already wear day-to-day — that's the whole point of casual
