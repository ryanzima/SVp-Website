Pre-Race Checklist

Who can join?

Delta Sigma Pi is open to all majors within the School of Management.

  • ✓

    Academic Standing

    Applicants must be in freshman, sophomore, or junior standing to join our fraternity.

  • ✓

    Academic Pursuit

    Applicants need to major or minor in one of the following disciplines to be eligible for membership: Business Administration, Accounting, Finance, Information Technology & Management, Entrepreneurship, or Economics.

  • ✓

    Academic Performance

    We require applicants to currently maintain at least a 2.75 GPA. If an applicant's GPA is lower than 2.75, we require them to submit a letter of statement.

  • ✓

    Attend required recruitment events

    Must attend at least 1 Meet the Chapter event and 2 additional recruitment events for eligibility.
